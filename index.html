Move your list between devices

Favorites and watchlist items do not sync automatically between iPhone and iPad. Export a backup to Files/iCloud Drive, then import it on your other device. Import replaces the existing saved list on that device.

Install on your Home Screen

After publishing this web app, open it in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen → enable “Open as Web App” → Add. Repeat on your other device.

Privacy & saved information

Watchlists, episode progress, favorite sources, recent links and playback positions are stored in this browser's local storage. Media stays on your device and is never sent to a server by this hub. Clearing Safari website data may erase your saved list. Back up before doing so.
